# Understanding Ethical Hacking

Ethical hacking means testing computers, websites, or networks with permission to find security weaknesses.

There are three types of hackers:

1. White Hat — Hackers who have permission to test security.
2. Black Hat — Hackers who break into systems illegally.
3. Grey Hat — Hackers who may find weaknesses without permission.

My goal is to learn ethical hacking and become a skilled cybersecurity professional.
